FolioTone

Read Docs Aloud

FolioTone reads your own documents aloud: PDF (including scanned PDFs), Word, EPUB, plain text and text in photos. Keep the original page in front of you while a built-in voice, an Apple system voice, or a voice created from a short recording of your own voice reads it to you.

Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing you import or record is uploaded.

小学声 · 看文读文

小学声（英文名 FolioTone）把你自己的文档读给你听：PDF（包括扫描版）、Word、EPUB、文本文件和照片里的文字。原文在眼前，可以选内置声音、Apple 系统声音，也可以录一小段自己的声音，用接近你的声音来读。

全部在这台 iPhone 上完成。不需要账号，没有广告，不追踪，你导入和录制的内容都不会上传。
